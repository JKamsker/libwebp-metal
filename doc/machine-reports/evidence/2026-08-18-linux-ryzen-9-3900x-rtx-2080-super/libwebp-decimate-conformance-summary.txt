Portable lossy-decimate conformance, issue #19
Date: 2026-08-19 UTC
Host: Ryzen 9 3900X / RTX 2080 SUPER (Turing, native sm_75 build)

Scope
- Versioned WPDCRD schema 1 over accelerator ABI 12.
- CPU-golden fixture capture; standalone loopback/CUDA/FPGA runner.
- Whole-pass and streaming/banded CUDA comparison.
- Transactional decline, timeout, error, and corrupt-checksum self-tests.
- End-to-end methods 3--6 bitstream parity and methods 2--6 tiny/odd matrix.
- Dependency-free FPGA descriptor that advertises and declines the stage.

Fixture suite
- 132 fixtures, 48,968 macroblocks.
- Methods 3--6; qualities 0, 25, 75, 98, and 100.
- Graphic, photo, texture, flat tie, and high-contrast inputs.
- 17x13 tiny, 257x255 odd, 512x512 aligned, and 33x31 partial-edge cases.
- All four segments, all 10 I4 modes, all four I16 modes, and all four UV
  modes were observed.
- 24,194 I4 and 24,774 I16 decisions; 8,570 zero-nz and 40,398 nonzero-nz
  macroblocks; nz union 0x01ffffff; coefficients ranged from -434 to 619.
- 120 error-diffusion fixtures, 80 banded fixtures, and 96 partial-edge
  fixtures.

Results
- CUDA WHOLE: 132/132 success and byte-exact result/reconstruction output.
- CUDA BEGIN/COLLECT: 132/132 success and byte-exact output.
- Every CUDA row reported nonzero, separately measured wavefront-execution and
  result-transfer times. These are diagnostic conformance timings, not an
  optimization or cross-hardware performance claim.
- End-to-end focused test: methods 3--6, qualities 75/99, one/two passes,
  padded strides, every band remainder, and forced collect/download fallback
  produced byte-identical WebP output.
- Tiny/odd matrix: 90/90 method/quality/input cells had identical CPU/CUDA
  SHA-256 and byte counts (methods 2--6, qualities 25/75/98, three content
  classes at 17x13 and 257x255).
- Portable self-test passed loopback success, decline, timeout, error,
  unchanged-output transactionality, checksum rejection, and FPGA decline.
- The portable self-test and an actual fixture load/loopback comparison also
  passed a Debug build with AddressSanitizer and UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer.
- A `BUILD_SHARED_LIBS=ON` CPU build linked and passed the same self-test; the
  opt-in private runner entry points have explicit shared-library visibility.
- All four CPU CTests passed. Seven targeted CUDA tests passed, including
  concurrency, near-lossless, the expanded trellis test, and conformance.
  The unrelated, pre-existing silent cuda_histogram_test failure was excluded
  from that retained transcript and remains documented by earlier experiments.
- A default CPU CMake build compiled neither lossy_decimate_fixture.c nor
  fpga_enc.c. Local Autotools execution was unavailable because autoreconf is
  not installed; the existing Correctness workflow retains Linux/macOS
  Autotools coverage and now builds the portable CMake self-test on both.

Oracle correction audit trail
- The first expanded oracle run was exact in 94/132 fixtures and exposed 38
  diffusion-tail mismatches in both whole and streaming modes.
- Method 5 publishes diffusion state during the basic UV search, then computes
  an intentionally discarded second value during selected-mode trellis.
  Quality 100 disables diffusion, leaving the ordinary CPU score tail inactive.
- Capture was corrected to reconstruct the state actually published to
  neighboring macroblocks and to canonicalize inactive bytes. The CUDA result
  itself already carried the published method-5 state. The retained 132/132
  reruns are in the final cuda-whole/cuda-streaming artifacts; the 94/132
  oracle-v1 rows and their manifests are retained for auditability.

Build/architecture guards
- CUDA configured with CMAKE_CUDA_ARCHITECTURES=native (cache retained).
- Ampere+ warm/cold defaults remain 64/4000 MBs.
- Pre-Ampere warm/cold defaults remain 784/12544 MBs.
- No architecture split, dispatch threshold, publication corpus, or
  publication generator was modified.
