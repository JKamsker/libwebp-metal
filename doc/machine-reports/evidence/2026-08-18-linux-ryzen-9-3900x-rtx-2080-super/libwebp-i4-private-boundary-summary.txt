Experiment: pre-Ampere warp-private I4 prediction boundaries
Machine: AMD Ryzen 9 3900X; NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2080 SUPER (sm_75)
Decision: rejected and removed

Fresh retained profile:
- PNG lossy: CPU 85.134 ms/image, CUDA 41.061 ms/image.
- JPEG lossy: CPU 85.822 ms/image, CUDA 39.555 ms/image.
- Medium photo/texture device walls: about 22--26 ms.
- Photo/texture I4 share: about 63--65% of block cycles.

Candidate:
- Replace the one shared 13-byte boundary per active I4 team with one exact
  private shared boundary per prediction warp.
- Each of four warp leaders gathers and immediately consumes its own boundary,
  removing the first CTA-wide barrier of every I4 dependency diagonal.
- Compile the candidate only for pre-Ampere; Ampere+ retains the parent path.

Validation:
- Candidate CTest: 7/7 passed.
- Restored-parent CTest: 7/7 passed.
- Timing rows: 24 (6 per format/arm), all hashes and byte counts exact.
- PNG output: d01c3571a90d2653, 1610422 bytes.
- JPEG output: befeef70bef22946, 1600198 bytes.

Pooled median batch time:
- PNG parent: 39.887852 ms/image.
- PNG candidate: 39.720484 ms/image.
- PNG gain: 0.167368 ms/image.
- JPEG parent: 39.404962 ms/image.
- JPEG candidate: 39.680605 ms/image.
- JPEG gain: -0.275643 ms/image.

Resources:
- Parent: 103 registers, 352-byte stack, 23392-byte shared memory.
- Candidate: 103 registers, 352-byte stack, 23464-byte shared memory.

Conclusion:
The removed CTA barrier does not repay four duplicate boundary gathers. PNG
is far below the 1.5 ms/image gate and JPEG regresses. The exact candidate was
removed. This is RTX 2080 SUPER-only performance evidence; Ampere+ behavior
was compile-time unchanged and no Ampere+ performance claim is made.
