  do not change Ampere+ behavior or decimation thresholds.

Protocol correction:
- Five order-balanced fresh process pairs per format were recorded, with one
  warmup and three retained 24-image samples per process.
- Every command used --force-cuda. ConfigureDispatch() runs after startup and
  unconditionally sets WEBP_CUDA_LOSSY_ANALYSIS=1 and
  WEBP_CUDA_FUSED_LOSSY_ANALYSIS=1.
- It therefore overwrote the requested analysis-off/fused-off baseline. The
  candidate's automatic default was also bypassed by the explicit value.
- All 60 rows exercised the same forced fused path. They are repeatability
  evidence and are not a valid parent/candidate timing comparison.

Correctness and restoration:
- Every one of the 60 timing rows retained its format's exact hash and bytes.
- Candidate and restored concurrency, trellis/padded-stride/band/fallback,
  and near-lossless focused CTests passed (3/3 each).
- The analysis kernel's earlier implementation validation already covered
  methods 0--6, qualities 25/75/95, odd dimensions, and CPU fallback; this

Decision:
Rejected and removed because the automatic policy is a no-op in the mandatory
forced-CUDA benchmark, which already explicitly enables ordinary and fused
analysis. The previously reported 0.200/-0.039 ms deltas are withdrawn. Keep
lossy analysis opt-in outside the benchmark. No Ampere+ behavior,
cross-hardware claim, or Turing/Ampere+ decimation threshold changed.
