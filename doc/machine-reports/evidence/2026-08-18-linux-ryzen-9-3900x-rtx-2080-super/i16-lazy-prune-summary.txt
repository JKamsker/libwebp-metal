Question
--------
Could the non-negative I16 residual cost be evaluated lazily? Mode 0 must be
evaluated. For later modes, the exact base score (distortion, spatial
distortion, fixed header cost, and the existing flatness chain) is a lower
bound on the full score. A mode whose base score already meets or exceeds the
best full score cannot win under the original strict-less tie rule.

Probe
-----
Temporary WEBP_CUDA_DECIMATE_TIMING=2 counters simulated that exact ordered
policy after the retained kernel had computed all four residual costs. The
probe counted how many mode residual walks a lazy implementation would need
and the resulting winner. It did not alter an encoder decision.

Command:
  WEBP_CUDA_DECIMATE_TIMING=2 \
    build-cuda-sm75-gate/webp_cuda_batch_benchmark \
    --variant cuda --mode lossy --batch-size 6 --method 4 --quality 75 \
    --warmups 0 --samples 1 --force-cuda \
    <photo-small/medium, texture-small/medium, graphic-small/medium PPM>

Results:
  photo-small:    4096 / 4096 evaluations; winners 0:1024
  photo-medium:   30000 / 30000 evaluations; winners 0:7500
  texture-small:  4096 / 4096 evaluations; winners 0:981, 2:26, 3:17
  texture-medium: 30000 / 30000 evaluations; winners 0:7374, 2:78, 3:48
  graphic-small:  3262 / 4096 evaluations (20.36% pruned)
                  winners 0:394, 1:231, 2:364, 3:35
  graphic-medium: 14757 / 30000 evaluations (50.81% pruned)
                  winners 0:5220, 1:1198, 2:1027, 3:55

Retention decision
------------------
Photo and texture, four of the six equally weighted suite images, cannot skip
any residual walk. For an intentionally optimistic upper bound, charge the
entire instrumented graphic I16-selection interval to residual walks and make
its cost scale perfectly with the pruned fraction:

  ((10.31 ms * 25.9% * 20.36%) +
   (27.56 ms * 30.3% * 50.81%)) / 6 = 0.80 ms/image

Real savings must be smaller because flatness, scalar selection, barriers, and
winner publication remain, and a lazy implementation introduces at least one
dependency/barrier after mode 0. The candidate's theoretical suite ceiling is
therefore already below the strict 1.5 ms/image retention threshold. No
candidate was implemented; the probe was removed and the retained source was
rebuilt with 7/7 CTests passing. This is Turing-only evidence and makes no
Ampere+ claim.
