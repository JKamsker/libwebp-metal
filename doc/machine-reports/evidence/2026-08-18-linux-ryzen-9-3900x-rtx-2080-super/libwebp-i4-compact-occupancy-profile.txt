Interpretation after timing
===========================
The medium corpus has at most 75 CTAs on a macroblock diagonal. Two CTAs per
each of 48 SMs already provide 96 resident slots, so the third residency slot
does not expose additional diagonal parallelism for this workload. Its small
latency-hiding benefit is below the project retention threshold.
