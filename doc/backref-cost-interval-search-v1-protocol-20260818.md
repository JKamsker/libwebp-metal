# Frozen backref-cost interval-search v1 protocol

The sole timing authority is one invocation of
`execute_backref_cost_interval_search_v1.py run` against `pandocs-agent` from
the exact clean phase-1 commit with `WEBP_BENCHMARK_SESSION=exclusive`.
Repository-context bundle verification, detached commit/tree and source hashes,
the canonical v1 path guard, admitted Unix-make/Xcode toolchain, host/power and
process checks, one nonblocking lease, synchronous children, indexed return,
and exact-child cleanup are conjunctive.

Before timing, three clean source clones are built with the frozen explicit
tools and SDK: default baseline, recorder-free candidate, and untimed recorder
candidate. The diagnostic clone records activation and direct list work for
the four timed cases at methods 4/6. In every active cell, candidate total
positioning steps must be at most 10% of baseline, activation and tail fast-path
counts must be positive, bitstreams must match, and all non-search counters
must be equal. The diagnostic binary refuses timed samples. It is discarded
before the timed matrix; neither timed binary contains recorder calls.

Correctness has 13 rows by methods 4/6: all nine publication cases at quality
75 and graphic-small at qualities 24, 25, 26, and 100. Every cell compares
default, candidate-off, candidate, repeat, injected pre-DP fallback, and
default-build-with-runtime-environment bitstreams, then decoded pixels against
the immutable generated input.

The performance matrix is frozen as follows:

- tune: photo-medium, graphic-medium;
- holdout: photo-large, graphic-large;
- methods: 4 and 6 independently;
- lifecycles: cold and warm independently;
- cold: 11 alternating process pairs, no warmup, one retained encode;
- warm: six alternating process pairs, two discarded warmups, nine retained;
- order: tune before holdout, photo before graphic, method 4 before 6, cold
  before warm, then fixed baseline/candidate alternation;
- inventory: 272 command processes, 176 cold samples, 192 warmups, 864 warm
  samples, 1,040 retained samples, 520 retained per variant, 16 evaluation
  cells, and eight required holdout cells.

Timing is runner-local `mach_continuous_time` around `WebPEncode` only. A cell
uses the median of paired candidate/baseline process medians and nearest-rank
raw p95 ratio. Tune is descriptive. Every holdout cell must have paired median
ratio at most 0.995 and p95 ratio at most 1.005. These thresholds and every
manifest hash are immutable after phase 1.

Promotion additionally requires every ancestry, identity, gate isolation,
default omission, ABI, correctness, work, source/toolchain, host, power,
process, resource, lease/release, archive/index/hash/extraction, transfer, and
exact-cleanup criterion. No workspace experiment sample or result may be
pooled, relabeled, or reused.

Every controlled exit writes a complete artifact index. The driver fetches and
hashes the lease record independently, inventories and safely extracts the
indexed archive all-or-nothing, removes only the exact validated child, and
returns an independent absence receipt. A refusal is terminal: no rerun,
retry, protocol edit, local timing fallback, threshold change, or executor
interpretation is allowed.
